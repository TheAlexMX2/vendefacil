# Surface Brief: VendeFácil Landing Page (`index.html`)

- **Scope:** Primary landing page (`index.html`, `src/App.jsx`, and its sections/modals).
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade
- **Audience & job:** Independent Mexican entrepreneurs and micro-merchants testing whether they can launch a credible mobile online store quickly for $99 MXN/mes with 0% platform commission and direct Mercado Pago checkout.
- **Primary action:** Customize the live store builder right in the first viewport and click "Publicar esta tienda por $99 MXN/mes".
- **Approved comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/decision/canon.png`
- **Craft bar:** Square / Clip, Shopify / Tiendanube, and Stripe / Mercado Pago (assumed after benchmark prompt was left unanswered).
- **Constraints:** Preserve VendeFácil brand name, $99 MXN/mes Plan Lite offer, 0% platform commission, Mercado Pago + WhatsApp flow, and all live WYSIWYG interactive features; label the 3 preset example stores honestly as interactive live demos (never fabricated real customers); no purple/violet AI SaaS clichés.

## Direction contract

THESIS: VendeFácil proves its $99 MXN/mes online store builder live in the first viewport with zero signup friction, refusing generic AI-SaaS purple gradients, glowing halos, and fabricated social proof in favor of calm, high-craft modern commerce clarity.

OWN-WORLD: Warm stone-white ground (#FAFAF9), pure-white elevated builder surface (#FFFFFF), crisp warm stone 1px hairlines (#E7E5E4), deep charcoal ink (#111827 / #4B5563), confident forest-teal actions (#0F766E / #115E59), measured geometric-humanist display and UI typography with tabular MXN numerals, and real warm studio product photography.

STORY: A Mexican independent merchant immediately understands the transparent $99 MXN/mes and 0% platform commission offer, tests their own brand name, subdomain, logo, store accent color, product photo, and MXN price in real time on the phone preview, trusts the Mercado Pago and WhatsApp workflow, and clicks to publish.

Sin código, sin comisiones." over subtitle "$99 MXN al mes · Cobra directo con Mercado Pago"; centered interactive workbench pairing a two-column white builder card (left 1–4 step rail + live Café Don Pedro form and full-width teal CTA "Publicar esta tienda por $99 MXN/mes") beside a dark-bezel live smartphone preview; anchored at the fold by a quiet 4-item trust strip (Mercado Pago integrado, 0% comisiones, SSL 256-bit, Notificaciones WhatsApp).
